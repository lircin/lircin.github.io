Glass, seasons and a workspace of your own

Sixteen glass foundations reshape the workspace backbone: directional rails, sculpted side panels, luminous bevels, reflected title bars and a quiet tactile finish. Vista Aero Glass now uses a much lighter tinted surface so the Windows desktop backdrop reads through the app, with a stronger glass highlight. Windows 2000 Professional uses crisp square controls, a classic two-tone bevel, a palette-tinted title strip and Tahoma. Desktop blur depends on Windows composition settings; screenshots show the app's themed surfaces, not a guaranteed desktop effect. Screenshots are genuine Windows app captures with fictional local demonstration content.

Also new: progressive video cut export, retained ignored-message history by date, safe temporary-cache cleanup, two independently hideable market tickers, sidebar-sized music with expand/restore, classic trout actions, connection traffic counters and opt-in CISA advisory links. AntiTroll is explicitly early beta. TCP packet loss is not measured; the advisory catalog is not a system scanner.
