Installs like real software.

A proper Setup wizard: welcome, license, folder, then a progress page that unpacks the application and its .NET and WPF libraries, adds Start-menu shortcuts and registers a clean uninstaller. On Linux, sh install.sh --wizard runs the same wizard under Wine, or installs the files directly.

Release files carry Sigstore signatures and SHA-256 checksums that prove integrity and workflow provenance. They are not Windows Authenticode-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn.

QUESTIONS

Good to know

Is it still regular IRC?

Yes. L'IRCin speaks standard IRC to any network. Extras such as encrypted DMs, games and voice work when both people use L'IRCin.

Why does Windows SmartScreen warn me?

Builds are not yet Authenticode-signed by a commercial certificate. Every release file has a Sigstore signature and SHA-256 checksum you can verify.

Does it run on Linux?

Experimental Wine packages run the Windows app. They are not native Linux builds.

What is FreeAds?

A separate, clearly labeled edition with a sponsor banner and tickers. The regular Free edition never shows ads.

Are the TV and sports streams hosted by L'IRCin?

No. The Stream tab links to official, authorized providers. Availability depends on your region and each provider.
