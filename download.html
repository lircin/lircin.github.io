First run

  1. Use the Setup wizard when provided, or unzip the portable package and run LIRCin.exe. No administrator access is required for a per-user installation.
  2. Current preview builds are unsigned. Windows may warn about an unrecognized publisher. Verify the source and checksum; a checksum alone does not establish publisher trust.
  3. Add a suggested network or your own server, then join a channel. Type /help for commands.

Verify your download

Each release lists a SHA-256 checksum. In PowerShell: Get-FileHash LIRCin-*.zip

Activate Supporter / Pro

After purchase, paste the license key from your Gumroad receipt in Edition & upgrade and choose Verify Gumroad purchase online, or enter /license YOUR-KEY. Gumroad verification does not consume a use. The verified key is protected for your Windows account and remains usable offline. Manually issued signed LIRC1- keys also continue to work offline.

Beta builds store settings in %APPDATA%\LIRCin – back it up before upgrading.
