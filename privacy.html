Privacy

L'IRCin has no accounts, no analytics and no license server. This site sets no cookies and loads no third-party trackers.

The app

  • Settings, logs and your saved passwords stay on your PC in %APPDATA%\LIRCin (passwords are protected with Windows DPAPI).
  • It connects only to the IRC servers you choose. The News panel fetches public headlines and market quotes; you can hide it with one click.
  • If “fetch profiles” is on, your nick's avatar is shared with other L'IRCin users over IRC. Turn it off in Settings.

This site

Hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visits per its own policy. The download page asks GitHub's public API for release information.

Purchases

Gumroad handles checkout under its own privacy policy. The seller uses your purchase details and contact information to verify purchases, issue signed license keys and provide support. License verification in the app remains offline.
