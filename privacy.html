Privacy

L'IRCin has no accounts, no analytics and no license server. This site sets no cookies and loads no third-party trackers.

The app

  • Settings, logs and your saved passwords stay on your PC in %APPDATA%\LIRCin (passwords are protected with Windows DPAPI).
  • IRC connections use the servers you choose. Optional features contact other services: News fetches headlines and quotes, Streams opens provider pages, and updates query GitHub. Video runtime downloads require consent. VirusTotal scanning and AI providers are separately configured; review their privacy implications before sending content.
  • If “fetch profiles” is on, your nick's avatar is shared with other L'IRCin users over IRC. Turn it off in Settings.

Optional FreeAds distribution (beta.22 preview)

FreeAds shows one labeled AdGuard affiliate banner outside conversations, plus contextual VPN/DNS links. Official images are bundled locally: displaying them sends no ad requests, impressions, chat content, nicknames or advertising identifiers. No ad scripts, cookies or tracking SDKs are loaded. The app no longer fetches remote campaign configuration automatically. Older sponsored versions may fetch public campaign configuration from GitHub.

Clicking an AdGuard link opens your browser with affiliate ID 138471; AdGuard and your browser may then use cookies and connection metadata under their own policies. Qualifying purchases may earn us a commission. Direct download links require confirmation; L'IRCin does not run or install affiliate software or change DNS automatically. AirVPN, Gumroad and other external destinations have their own policies. Free without ads has no affiliate placements; paid activation hides them immediately.

This site

Hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visits per its own policy. The download page asks GitHub's public API for release information.

Purchases

Gumroad handles checkout under its own privacy policy. When you activate a Gumroad license, L'IRCin sends the license key and product identifier directly to Gumroad over HTTPS for purchase verification without incrementing its use count. The verified key is protected using Windows Data Protection for your Windows account and is not sent during normal offline use. Manually issued signed LIRC1 keys remain locally verifiable without a network request.
